Owen Peterson

I recently graduated from the University of Minnesota with a degree in Computer Science and a minor in Statistics.

Building software is like building a tool for a job site in that it only matters if it actually makes the work easier.

And leading a crew taught me that most problems are communication problems, so whether I'm setting the plan or just executing someone else's, I make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

When I'm away from my keyboard, you can find me biking or playing basketball, at the gym, sketching, journaling, or unwinding with music and a good book.
